Dynamic Modelling & Control of a Vanadium Redox Flow Battery Interfaced via Bidirectional DC-DC Converter

MATLAB / Simulink Simscape Battery Power Electronics State of Charge (SoC) DC-Bus Regulation
Situation

Grid-scale renewable storage demands long-duration solutions, but Vanadium Redox Flow Batteries (VRFBs) exhibit non-linear terminal voltage dynamics dictated by electrolyte flow rates, stack electrochemistry, and auxiliary pumping losses.

Task

Investigate the dynamic behaviour and design closed-loop control strategies for a VRFB interfaced to a DC bus through a bidirectional DC-DC converter in MATLAB/Simulink (Simscape Battery).

Action

Extended dynamic electrochemical stack models with bidirectional converter topologies; implementing multi-loop current, power, and DC-bus voltage controllers across charging/discharging cycles.

Result

Characterizing stack efficiency across variable electrolyte flow rates and validating DC-bus voltage stability under dynamic grid step-load transients.

Industrial Automation & Energy Optimization

Smart Drive System & Advanced Process Control for Pit Dewatering (Exxaro Grootegeluk)

Advanced Process Control (APC) Variable Speed Drives (VSD) Hydraulic Affinity Laws Scope 2 Emissions Abatement
Situation

Grootegeluk Mine's open-cast pit dewatering (Bench 13) relied on binary Direct-on-Line (DOL) starters, subjecting pipelines to severe hydraulic water-hammer shocks, high inrush currents, and massive energy waste.

Task

Formulate an automation and motor control proposal integrating Variable Speed Drives (VSDs) and an APC multivariable control layer to optimize pumping, protect assets, and reduce Scope 2 carbon emissions.

Action

Specified 4x WEG CFW11 VSDs (280 kW / 600 V) with pipeline pressure transmitters; designed an APC Finite State Machine with fault detection (cavitation, pipe burst, sedimentation); modeled energy savings via Hydraulic Affinity Law 3.

Result

Demonstrated annual electrical energy savings of 4.32 GWh across 4 pumps, yielding R5.21M annual OPEX savings, a R1.11M SARS Section 12L tax rebate, and 100% capital payback within Year 1.

Micromouse X Multi-Rail Power Management & Motor Driver PCB

Altium / KiCad Buck-Boost (TPS63020) Dual H-Bridge (DRV8833) I2C Current Sensing (INA219)

An autonomous maze-solving Micromouse robot required a compact ($82 \times 60\text{ mm}$), isolated power PCB capable of driving 4 motors bidirectionally, switching high-side loads, and monitoring battery health under a strict $70 budget.

Task

Design, route, fabricate, and acceptance-test a multi-channel power and actuator driver board meeting strict thermal and footprint constraints.

Action

Autonomous Sea-Ice Vertical Temperature & Conductive Heat Flux Profiler

STM32F411 (Black Pill) 1-Wire DS18B20 Chain Fourier Heat Flux Independent Watchdog (IWDG) Brown-Out Reset (BOR)
Situation

Understanding Antarctic air-ice-ocean thermodynamic decay requires continuous in-situ vertical thermal profiling in the Marginal Ice Zone (MIZ), but harsh sub-zero temperatures and power limits risk system freezes and data corruption.

Task

Engineer the Data Processing & Integrity firmware on an STM32 microcontroller to acquire multi-depth thermistor profiles, calculate spatial conductive heat flux, and guarantee 14-day autonomous fault recovery.

Action

Addressed a 6-node DS18B20 1-Wire sensor string by unique 64-bit hardware ROM; implemented 1D Fourier Heat Conduction math ($q = -k\frac{\Delta T}{\Delta x}$); integrated RTC interrupt sampling, a 3-point moving-average filter, NAN error-masking for severed lines, and RCC_CSR register polling for IWDG/BOR hardware reset auditing.

Result

Achieved post-calibration temperature accuracy within $\pm0.1^{\circ}\text{C}$, verified deterministic 13-field CSV packet streaming to ESP32 telemetry, and validated low-power execution ($I_{\text{Active}}=26.6\text{ mA}$, $I_{\text{Sleep}}=7.82\text{ mA}$).

Digital Logic & Hardware Acceleration

CORDIC Coordinate Rotation Unit Co-Processor for StarCore-1 RISC CPU

Verilog HDL RISC Architecture Fixed-Point Shift-and-Add Icarus Verilog & GTKWave
Situation

The 16-bit single-cycle StarCore-1 RISC CPU lacked native transcendental support, requiring slow, multi-cycle software Taylor-series approximations (>100 cycles) for trigonometric functions in real-time embedded robotics.

Task

Design, integrate, and verify a hardware Coordinate Rotation Unit (CRU) co-processor executing 16-iteration CORDIC micro-rotations in synthesizable Verilog.

Action

Implemented a 4-state FSM (IDLE, LOAD, ITERATE, DONE) using arithmetic right-shifts and a precomputed arctangent LUT; extended the CPU Control Unit with opcodes (`COS`: 1010, `SIN`: 1110), pipeline stall logic, and a 3-way write-back multiplexer; validated against a 64-bit Python golden reference.

Result

Achieved single trigonometric execution in exactly 19 clock cycles (190 ns @ 100 MHz; 5.3M ops/s) with maximum absolute error $< 2^{-13}$ across $[-\pi/2, \pi/2]$, requiring only ~150 LUTs for FPGA synthesis.

Control Systems & Mechatronics

Robust Digital Cascade Controller for Multi-Configuration DC Servo Motor

MATLAB / Simulink Quantitative Feedback Theory (QFT) Cascade Architecture System Identification
Situation

Precision speed and position regulation of a DC servo motor is compromised by non-linear dead-bands ($\pm1.7\text{ V}$), DAC saturation ($\pm10\text{ V}$), and physical plant variations across 4 distinct inertia/damping loads (LD, LB, HB, HD).

Task

Perform plant system identification and design a robust digital cascade controller satisfying strict frequency and time-domain bounds (settling time $< 1.0\text{ s}$, overshoot $< 20\%$, digital phase margin $> 15^{\circ}$).

Action

Identified first-order LTI plant gains and sensor constants via linear least-squares regression; applied QFT loop shaping on an inner-loop proportional speed controller ($G_i$) to reject inertia lag before designing an outer-loop lead compensator ($G_o$); refined $G_i=3.5$ on physical hardware to eliminate bang-bang DAC saturation.

Result

Verified robust setpoint tracking and disturbance rejection ($\le -20\text{ dB} @ 0.5\text{ rad/s}$) across all plant configurations in Simulink and physical bench implementation.
