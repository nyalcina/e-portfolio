Industrial Automation & Control

Advanced Process Control & Pit Dewatering Optimization

MATLAB / Simulink Variable Speed Drives (VSD) Hydraulics Feedback Control

Situation: Open-cast pit dewatering operations experience high pump wear and energy losses due to fixed-speed pumping under variable head pressures.

Task: Design an Advanced Process Control (APC) strategy integrating Variable Speed Drives to dynamically regulate flow rates and minimize power consumption.

Action: Modeled nonlinear pipe friction and pump head-capacity curves; implemented closed-loop feedback algorithms modulating VSD frequency based on sump inflow.

Result: Achieved steady-state tracking error under 2% while reducing simulated peak energy consumption and mitigating hydraulic hammer shocks.
