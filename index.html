Dynamic Modelling & Control of a Vanadium Redox Flow Battery Interfaced via Bidirectional DC-DC Converter

MATLAB / Simulink Simscape Battery Power Electronics State of Charge (SoC) DC-Bus Regulation
Situation

Grid-scale renewable storage demands long-duration solutions, but flow battery terminal voltage varies non-linearly with electrolyte flow rates, stack chemistry, and pump losses.

Task

Model the dynamic electrochemical behavior of a multi-cell VRFB and design closed-loop bidirectional DC-DC converter controllers for stable DC-bus integration.

Industrial Automation & Energy Optimization

Smart Drive System & Advanced Process Control (Exxaro Grootegeluk)

Advanced Process Control (APC) Variable Speed Drives (VSD) Hydraulic Affinity Laws Scope 2 Emissions
Situation

Open-cast pit dewatering on Bench 13 relied on binary Direct-on-Line starters, causing hydraulic water-hammer shocks, pipe fatigue, and high electrical energy costs.

Task

Develop an automation proposal using Variable Speed Drives and an APC control layer to automate pumping, protect pipe infrastructure, and cut Scope 2 carbon emissions.

Action

Specified four 280 kW / 600 V WEG CFW11 VSDs; designed an APC state machine with automated fault detection (cavitation, pipe burst); modeled savings using Hydraulic Affinity Law 3.

Result

Demonstrated 4.32 GWh annual energy savings across 4 pumps, yielding R5.21M annual OPEX savings, a R1.11M SARS Section 12L tax rebate, and 100% payback in Year 1.

Embedded Systems & Polar Instrumentation

Autonomous Sea-Ice Vertical Temperature & Conductive Heat Flux Profiler

STM32F411 (Black Pill) 1-Wire DS18B20 Chain Fourier Heat Flux Watchdog (IWDG) Brown-Out Reset (BOR)
Situation

Monitoring Antarctic sea-ice melt requires continuous in-situ vertical thermal profiling in the Marginal Ice Zone, where freezing temperatures risk system lockups and data corruption.

Task

Develop the Data Processing & Integrity firmware on an STM32 microcontroller to acquire multi-depth temperatures, calculate conductive heat flux, and ensure autonomous recovery.

Action

Sampled a 6-node DS18B20 1-Wire string by unique 64-bit ROM; calculated Fourier heat flux; implemented moving-average filtering, NAN fault-masking, and RCC_CSR register polling for IWDG/BOR hardware reset auditing.

Result

Achieved post-calibration temperature accuracy within +/-0.1 deg C, validated 13-field CSV packet streaming to telemetry, and confirmed low-power duty cycling (26.6 mA active, 7.82 mA sleep).

Digital Logic & Hardware Acceleration

CORDIC Coordinate Rotation Unit Co-Processor for StarCore-1 RISC CPU

Verilog HDL RISC Architecture Fixed-Point Shift-and-Add Icarus Verilog & GTKWave
Situation

The 16-bit single-cycle StarCore-1 RISC CPU lacked native trigonometric hardware, requiring slow, multi-cycle software approximations (>100 cycles) for robotic coordinate transforms.

Task

Design, synthesize, and verify a tightly coupled 16-iteration CORDIC co-processor in Verilog HDL to accelerate sine and cosine calculations in hardware.

Action

Implemented a 4-state FSM using shift-and-add arithmetic and an integer arctangent LUT; extended the CPU Control Unit with custom opcodes, pipeline stall logic, and a 3-way write-back multiplexer; verified against a Python golden model.

Result

Computed trigonometric outputs in 19 clock cycles (190 ns @ 100 MHz) with error under 2^-13 across [-pi/2, pi/2], requiring only ~150 LUTs for FPGA implementation.

Control Systems & Mechatronics

Robust Digital Cascade Controller for Multi-Configuration DC Servo Motor

MATLAB / Simulink Quantitative Feedback Theory (QFT) Cascade Architecture System Identification
Situation

Speed and position control of a DC servo motor is hindered by static dead-bands (+/-1.7 V), DAC saturation (+/-10 V), and varying inertial loads across 4 configurations (LD, LB, HB, HD).

Task

Perform empirical system identification and synthesize a robust digital cascade controller meeting strict time and frequency-domain bounds (settling time < 1.0 s, overshoot < 20%).

Action

Derived plant transfer functions using least-squares regression; performed QFT loop shaping on an inner speed controller (Gi = 3.5) and outer position compensator (Go); tuned hardware parameters to eliminate bang-bang actuator saturation.

Result

Verified robust setpoint tracking and disturbance rejection (<= -20 dB at 0.5 rad/s) across all plant configurations in Simulink and physical laboratory testing.
