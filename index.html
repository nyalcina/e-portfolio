Power Electronics & Storage Final Year Project · In Progress

Vanadium Redox Flow Battery & Bidirectional DC-DC Interface

Power Electronics MATLAB & Simulink State of Charge (SoC) Bidirectional Converters
Situation

Grid-scale renewable storage requires durable solutions, but electrochemical dynamics of flow batteries introduce complex non-linear voltage and SoC characteristics.

Task

Model and simulate a multi-cell Vanadium Redox Flow Battery (VRFB) and control its integration via a bidirectional DC-DC converter topology.

Action

Formulated electrochemical equivalent-circuit models in MATLAB/Simulink; designing closed-loop voltage/current controllers to regulate dynamic charging/discharging cycles.

Result

Characterizing electrolyte flow rate effects on stack efficiency and validating converter bus regulation under step load variations.

Industrial Automation & Control

Advanced Process Control & Pit Dewatering Optimization

Control Systems Variable Speed Drives Dynamic Hydraulics Process Optimization
Situation

High-volume pit dewatering operations experience significant mechanical pump wear and energy losses due to fixed-speed pumping under dynamic hydraulic heads.

Task

Develop an Advanced Process Control (APC) strategy integrating Variable Speed Drives (VSD) to dynamically regulate flow rates and minimize energy demand.

Action

Modeled nonlinear pipe network friction and pump head-capacity curves; implemented closed-loop feedback algorithms to modulate VSD frequency based on real-time sump inflows.

Result

Achieved steady-state tracking error under 2% while reducing simulated pump peak power demand and mitigating water-hammer hydraulic shocks.

Digital Logic & Hardware Acceleration

CORDIC Trigonometric Co-Processor in Verilog HDL

Verilog HDL ModelSim / FPGA RISC Bus Architecture Fixed-Point Arithmetic
Situation

General-purpose microcontrollers incur significant clock-cycle overhead when computing trigonometric functions via software approximation libraries.

Task

Design, verify, and interface a 16-bit pipelined CORDIC hardware accelerator for a RISC microprocessor core.

Action

Built finite state machines for vectoring and rotation modes using shift-and-add arithmetic; authored ModelSim testbenches to verify convergence across all quadrants.

Result

Achieved accurate angle and vector rotations within <1% theoretical error, dramatically reducing execution latency compared to software math routines.
